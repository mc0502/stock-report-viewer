5. Decision Checklist — Before Every Trade

Run through these 13 steps in order before opening any position suggested by this report. The goal is to prevent acting on noise. Step 11 (IV term-structure check) was added in v8.6; step 2 now references the macro layer.

1
Check the alert banner first — If the orange alert banner is visible at the top of the report, read every item before opening any card. ⚡ D1 = price moved hard (check catalyst). ⬟ D2 = signal fired (find the card immediately). 📅 D3 = calendar event (check earnings / upgrade date).
2
Market regime — Check the regime banner below the alert banner. In Bear or High-Vol regimes, skip bullish setups unless InvScore ≥ 70 AND confidence is High. All scores are already regime-adjusted, but add your own filter on top. Also check yield curve (inverted = macro headwind), Fed regime badge, and breadth score in the regime banner — these now feed directly into InvScore Layer 5 Macro (v8.6).
3
Earnings banner present? — If yes → stop. Do not open new positions into a binary earnings event. If you hold, tighten your stop to the nearest support level.
4
Direction + Confidence — Only act on BUY/SELL with High or Medium confidence. Low confidence = signal noise. HOLD = no edge detected.
5
Score bars: both green? — Use the score bars (always visible) for a quick pre-filter. Both bars green (≥65) = both layers confirm quality. One bar red = investigate before entering. InvScore now has 5 real layers — tech, graph, news, fundamentals, AND macro/options context. A score of 65+ means multiple layers are genuinely positive.
6
InvScore ≥ 65 — InvScore < 65 = at least one major data layer is weak. Best entries happen when InvScore ≥ 65 and Signal Score ≥ 60 and they agree on direction.
7
Conflict or 52W Low flag? — If the Pattern Breakdown panel shows a conflict flag, the core patterns disagree — halve your normal position size. If the 52W Low flag is shown, do not buy — wait for a reversal candle with volume.
8
EMA200 macro filter — Price below EMA200 = macro bearish context. Only take BUY signals if InvScore ≥ 70 AND there is a clear catalyst. Otherwise, confine to short or neutral positions.
9
RVOL-ToD > 1.0? — Volume below average on a breakout = lack of conviction. Wait for RVOL ≥ 1.0 before entering. RVOL ≥ 1.5 = institutional participation, highest-quality setups.
10
ADX > 20? — If ADX < 20, the market is ranging. Trend-following signals (EMA stack, MACD cross) are unreliable in ranging markets. Favour mean-reversion setups (Williams %R extremes) instead.
11
IV Term Structure Warning present? — ⚡ badge = large open interest expiring this week (v8.6, I13). If put-heavy → heightened downside pin risk. If call-heavy → watch for a gamma squeeze. Do not sell weekly options without checking this.
12
News — sanity check only — Read the headlines to understand why the stock is moving. Do not trade on news sentiment alone. 1–3 keyword-matched articles ≠ a reliable signal. The news layer carries only 9% of InvScore weight for this reason.
13
Check your own risk rules last — Apply your personal position-sizing rule (e.g. max 2% risk per trade), set a stop-loss at the nearest structural level (usually just below EMA50), and log the trade with the Signal Score + InvScore at entry for review.

🌐 Market Breadth Regime (v8.3)

A 6-indicator composite (-6 to +6) combining: RSP/SPY equal-weight ratio, sector ETF participation (% of 8 sectors above 200MA), SPY EMA200 slope, VIX level & trend, IWM/QQQ small-cap breadth, and cross-asset signals (TLT bonds, GLD gold). Mapped to regime multiplier: Strong Bull=1.0×, Bull=0.90×, Neutral=0.75×, Weak=0.55×, Bear=0.35×.

CAPE modifier: SPY P/E >35 (historically 99th percentile) compresses conviction by 0.90× — reflecting Gorman et al. (2025) finding that 10-year forward returns from extreme valuations average ~3% annualised.

Duration filter: Regime only switches after 3 consecutive runs agree (Jegadeesh & Titman 1993). Pending transitions shown as "OldLabel (→NewLabel)" in the header.

After 20+ evaluated signals accumulate in signal_log.csv, the system automatically recalibrates sector weight_boost values in user_feedback.json using real outcome data. Methodology: McLean-Pontiff (2016) decay haircut, Harvey-Liu-Zhu (2016) n≥30 pattern gate, López de Prado (2018) walk-forward trend, Bailey et al. (2014) PBO≥20 guard. Calibration runs weekly if enough new signals exist.

⚠ Drawdown Recovery Asymmetry (v8.3)

Per Gorman et al. (2025): a 50% drawdown requires a 100% gain to break even — approximately 10.2 years at 7% CAGR. Recovery time estimates shown per ticker use the formula: years = log(1 + required_return) / log(1 + 0.07). This is the core asymmetry argument for loss avoidance over return maximisation.
